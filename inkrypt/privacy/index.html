Privacy Policy — Inkrypt

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Developer: Roncon — contact: ronconapps@gmail.com

This policy covers the Inkrypt app (package com.roncon.inkrypt), a single-player cryptography puzzle game.

1. Summary

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your progress stays on your device. We do not receive it, and we run no servers for the game.
  • The app has no advertising and no analytics, and it does not send crash reports.
  • The only online part is the optional Volume II purchase, handled by Google Play and Unity In-App Purchasing.

2. Information the app handles

Kept only on your device. Your progress: letters solved, wax seals, Standing and rank, Ink and hint credits, unlocked Ink colours, notebook entries, settings, and a record of whether you own Volume II. This is stored in the app's private storage. We do not receive it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Sent to us. Nothing. The app does not contact any server run by us. If you choose "Send feedback" in Settings, your email app opens with a draft addressed to us; what you send is up to you, and we use it only to reply and improve the game.

3. Third-party services

Google Play. Payments for Volume II are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. The app learns only whether you own Volume II, and it can ask Google Play to restore a past purchase. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Unity In-App Purchasing. The purchase library from Unity sends purchase information to Unity when you start, complete, fail or restore a purchase. This can include the product, price and currency, a transaction identifier, and device details such as your IP address and a device identifier. Unity processes it under its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

Unity engine. The game is built with the Unity engine.

Links. The Settings screen can open this page, the app's Google Play page or your email app. Those open outside the app and follow their own policies.

4. Why we use information

  • to let you save your progress on your device
  • to process the Volume II purchase and restore it if you reinstall
  • to answer feedback you send us

5. Retention

On your device: until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Feedback emails: kept as long as needed to reply. Google and Unity keep purchase data under their own policies.

6. Security

Purchase requests use HTTPS. Your progress is stored on your device only, so it is protected by your device's own security. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases are made through Google Play, which has its own parental controls.

8. Your choices and rights

You can uninstall the app to delete everything stored on your device, and you can decide not to buy Volume II. Because we hold no data about you, we cannot look up or delete information about a specific person; for purchase data, contact Google or Unity. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access or delete data held about you, and you can email us with any request.

9. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date. If a later version adds advertising, analytics or crash reporting, this policy will say so before it ships.
