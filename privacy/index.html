Privacy Policy — Hold Your Fire

Effective date: 15 November 2026

Developer: Roncon — contact: ronconapps@gmail.com

This policy covers the Hold Your Fire app (package com.roncon.holdyourfire) on Android and iOS.

1. Summary

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number or precise location.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • When the app goes online, our servers receive your IP address and, if you play online, the display name you choose and your game inputs.
  • The app uses Google AdMob (ads), Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports), and Google Play or the App Store with Unity In-App Purchasing (the "Remove Ads" purchase).

2. Information the app handles

Kept only on your device. Your Honor, progress and match records, settings (display name, controls, sound, region) and whether you own Remove Ads. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Sent to our game servers.

  • Each time the app starts, and when you open online play, it contacts our room service to check availability, the server region and the required app version. This happens even if you only play offline, and your device's IP address reaches our servers, as it does for any internet connection.
  • When you play online: the display name you choose (or a random name the app suggests), your platform (for example "Android"), the room settings you pick, and your game inputs during the match. Other players in your room see your display name, and public rooms show the host's name to people browsing rooms. There is no chat.
  • Match traffic is not encrypted. Your display name and game inputs travel without encryption during a match. Do not use your real name as a display name.

Server logs. Our web server keeps access logs, which include IP addresses, for up to 30 days. Our game-service logs are kept for up to 30 days and contain no IP addresses. Our room servers keep logs of recent rooms, which include the display names of the players and no IP addresses. These logs are limited by number, keeping the most recent 200 rooms, not by age, so they can be kept for longer than 30 days when few rooms are played.

Where data is processed. Our game servers are located in India. Google and Unity process data on their own infrastructure, which may be in other countries.

3. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising). The app shows full-screen ads between matches unless you own Remove Ads. Each time the app starts, Google's User Messaging Platform checks whether a consent choice is required where you live, for example in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, and if so asks for it. Where ads run, Google may collect and share your IP address, advertising ID, app set ID, app interactions such as app launches, taps and ad views, and diagnostic information, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and limit ad personalisation, in your device settings. Where the app offers a privacy options entry, you can change your consent choice there. Google's information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Firebase Crashlytics (crash and error reports, by Google). When the app crashes or records an error, Google receives:

  • the stack trace and the app's state
  • device details such as model, operating system and memory
  • a Firebase installation ID and a Crashlytics installation identifier
  • a few labels we attach: app version, platform, the screen you were on, why your last online session ended, and the number of low-memory warnings

We attach no display names, room codes or purchase details. Crash reporting is on by default, and there is no in-app switch. Google keeps crash data for 90 days before removal begins. Installation IDs are deleted after 270 days of inactivity. More information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Purchases. Payments are handled by Google Play on Android and by the App Store on iOS. We never see your card or bank details. The app learns only whether you own Remove Ads.

Unity In-App Purchasing. The purchase library from Unity sends purchase event information to Unity when you start, complete or fail a purchase, and when a pending or restored purchase is processed. This information includes the product, price and currency, transaction and purchase identifiers, the purchase receipt, and device details such as model, operating system and language. The app does not send your advertising ID to Unity. Unity's information: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Unity engine. The game is built with the Unity engine.

4. Why we use information

We use information to:

  • run online rooms and matches
  • check service availability, the server region and the required app version
  • protect the service from abuse, for example with request limits
  • find and fix crashes
  • show ads and let you remove them
  • process the Remove Ads purchase

5. Retention

  • On your device: until you uninstall the app.
  • A room: exists only while it runs.
  • Server logs: see section 2.
  • Crashlytics: see section 3.
  • Google (AdMob, Play) and Unity: they keep data under their own policies. We do not control their retention periods.

6. Security

Requests to our room service and to Google and Unity services use HTTPS. Match traffic is not encrypted (see section 2). Our servers limit request rates and expose only the room API publicly. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app online, contact us at ronconapps@gmail.com. We will remove matching information from our logs where we can identify it, for example by display name and approximate time.

8. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • reset your advertising ID in your device settings
  • change your ad consent where the app offers a privacy options entry
  • buy Remove Ads to stop ads
  • choose any display name
  • uninstall the app to delete local data

We do not link server data to an account or to any identifier you could give us, so we may not be able to find information about a specific person. Logs are deleted automatically as described in section 2. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal information. Contact ronconapps@gmail.com and we will do what we can with the information we hold.
