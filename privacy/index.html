Hold Your Fire - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

This policy describes what the game "Hold Your Fire" (the "app"), published by Roncon ("we"), does with information. Contact: ronconapps@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your player name, your in-game "Honor" total, your chosen settings (for example controls and arena) and advertising frequency counters on your device. This data stays on the device unless stated below.

Online play

If you play online, the app connects to our game servers. To run a match we receive the player name you choose, the room code, your in-game inputs and your device's network address (IP address), which is needed to deliver the connection. Other players in the same room see your player name. Please do not use your real name or personal information as a player name. Technical server logs that may contain this information are kept for up to 1 month and then deleted.

Advertising

The app may show ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested, and you can change your choice later from the PRIVACY button on the title screen when it is shown. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

What we do not do

We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not sell your personal information.

Children

Hold Your Fire is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact ronconapps@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can delete the data on your device by uninstalling the app. To ask about data held on our servers, contact us at the address above. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
